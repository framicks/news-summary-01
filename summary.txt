World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: Russia and Ukraine agreed their largest prisoner swap after two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, with numbers due Thursday; there was no ceasefire or deal (Reuters). World leaders opened the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, where 102 world leaders were expected; the US sent no federal delegation for a second year (Reuters). Two tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said, as Britain's maritime agency reported another tanker struck; Brent crude rose above $92 (Reuters). Indonesia's Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka tendered his resignation to President Prabowo, after a court annulled his school certificate (Reuters). Democrat Scott Colom won Mississippi's Senate race by 4,669 votes, the AP called on Wednesday, becoming the state's first Black senator since 1881 and its first Democrat in the Senate since 1982 (AP). Saudi air defences intercepted Houthi missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh and Mecca, the coalition said; the Houthis denied targeting Mecca (Reuters). Ebola in DR Congo passed 11,000 cases and 5,300 deaths, government data showed, the second-largest outbreak on record, with about 3,000 recovered (AP). Emailed bomb threats reached some 6,300 Italian schools, forcing evacuations at hundreds of them; similar threats went to schools in Scotland, Northern Ireland and Ireland, and PM Giorgia Meloni said they posed no danger (Reuters).
